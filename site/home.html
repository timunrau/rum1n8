How it works

1. Add a verse

You can add a single verse, a range of verses, or even an entire chapter. Verse text is imported automatically from supported Bible translations.

2. Learn, Memorize, Master

Type the first letter of each word as you progress through three stages of memorization.

3. Review daily

Ruminate uses spaced repetition to schedule reviews based on how well you know each verse.

Frequently
asked questions

Can I install Ruminate on my phone?

Yes. You can install Ruminate from the website and open it from your home screen like any other app. It’s also available on Google Play.

Can I use Ruminate on multiple devices?

Yes. You can sync your verses and progress across devices using your own Google Drive.

Can I back up my verses?

Yes. You can download all your verses, progress, and settings as a plain text file. Your data is yours to keep, edit, or convert into other formats.

Can I import my verses?

Yes. You can import your verses from a CSV file or use our BibleMemory.com importer.

Can I use Ruminate offline?

Yes. You can memorize and review your saved verses without an internet connection.
